## Draf jawaban (English)
**Company name:** Sebooth · **Website:** https://sebooth.in

**Describe your product:**
Sebooth runs photobooth hardware at Indonesian events and a self-built platform at sebooth.in: guests scan a QR to instantly claim their photos, strips, GIFs and videos, and organizers use a digital queue with push notifications and an admin console. 3,460+ sessions processed to date.

**How do you plan to use the OpenAI API?**
- Image generation to draft event-themed photostrip frames from an organizer's brief, reviewed by our designers before use
- Vision models to pick best shots and write captions for each event gallery, producing a recap pack for organizers
- A booking assistant on our website and WhatsApp, grounded on our pricing and FAQ data, that answers questions and drafts quotes
- Drafting support for our local SEO content, with human editing before publishing
